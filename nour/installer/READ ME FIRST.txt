NOUR AI — how to install (one time)

1. Right-click "Install Nour AI.command" → Open → Open.
   (Right-click is needed once because this installer isn't from the App Store.)
2. A Terminal window installs everything (about 5–10 minutes the first time).
3. When it says "Nour AI is installed", Nour opens by itself.

After that: open "Nour AI" from your Applications folder (Home → Applications),
or drag it to your Dock. Tap the Nour logo and talk. Chrome gives the best voice.

Smarter answers: in Nour, open the ^ menu → Connected apps / Settings, or pick a bigger model
such as qwen3.5:9b in the classic OpenJarvis page (http://127.0.0.1:8000/). Ollama runs it on your Mac.

To update Nour AI later, run the installer again.
